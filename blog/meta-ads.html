10 Proven Meta Ads Strategies That Generated 5X ROAS in 2024

Scaling a business using Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) has never been more challenging—or more rewarding. After managing over ₹10 Crore in ad spend across more than 200 campaigns, our team at Internet Spark has cracked the code. We’ve discovered what separates campaigns that burn budget from those that consistently deliver a 5X Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

1. Broad Audience Targeting is the New Gold Standard

Gone are the days of hyper-granular interest targeting. Meta’s machine learning algorithm is incredibly smart. By using broader audiences and letting the algorithm find your buyers based on the creative, we’ve seen CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) drop by up to 35%.

2. The "3-Second Hook" Creative Framework

Users scroll through their feeds at lightning speed. If your video ad doesn’t capture attention in the first 3 seconds, you’ve lost them. We start every video ad with a bold statement, a surprising visual, or a direct call-out to the target audience's biggest pain point.

3. Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC)

For our E-commerce clients, switching to Meta’s Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns has been a game-changer. By consolidating campaigns and letting AI handle the heavy lifting of audience targeting and creative combinations, our average ROAS increased from 2.5X to 5.2X.

4. User-Generated Content (UGC) Dominates

Highly polished, studio-quality ads often perform worse than authentic, smartphone-shot UGC. People want to buy from people. Ads that look like native social media posts build instant trust and drastically lower the CPC (Cost Per Click).

5. Retargeting with Irresistible Offers

The majority of users won’t buy on their first interaction. We segment our retargeting audiences based on their engagement (e.g., added to cart, viewed 75% of video) and hit them with time-sensitive, irresistible offers that push them over the buying line.

💡 Pro Tip from Internet Spark

Stop turning off ads after just 24 hours! Meta’s algorithm needs a minimum of 50 conversion events within a 7-day window to fully optimize. Patience is a crucial part of the strategy.

6. Copywriting: Benefits Over Features

Your ad copy shouldn't just list product features; it must sell the transformation. We focus heavily on the emotional payoff and the end result the customer will achieve by using the product or service.

7. Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO)

Instead of guessing which image goes best with which headline, we feed Meta 3 images, 2 videos, 3 headlines, and 2 primary texts using DCO. Meta automatically tests all combinations and shifts budget to the winning variation.

8. Frictionless Landing Pages

A great ad is useless if it sends traffic to a slow or confusing landing page. We ensure that our clients' landing pages load in under 2.5 seconds, have clear CTA buttons above the fold, and match the exact messaging of the ad.

9. Simplified Account Structure

Over-segmenting ad sets leads to audience fragmentation and learning phase resets. We use a simplified account structure (typically 1 Prospecting Campaign and 1 Retargeting Campaign) to feed more data into fewer ad sets, speeding up optimization.

10. Data-Driven Scaling Rules

Scaling too fast can break an ad's performance. When an ad hits our target ROAS, we scale the budget horizontally (duplicating into new audiences) or vertically (increasing budget by a maximum of 20% every 48 hours) to maintain stability.
